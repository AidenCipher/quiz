## Things to know

- Finished-game results are keyed by nickname only. A player who asks for deletion after the game can only be found by nickname and approximate time, which the page says honestly; the host can also delete the results.
- Deleting a host account does not interrupt a game that is already live; the room is unreachable for new connections and is cleaned up by its timer.
- Cloudflare (hosting) and Google (host sign-in) are processors/independent controllers under their own terms; the policies name them.
- The retention figures in the policies mirror constants in `apps/worker/src/room.ts` (cleanup timers) and the daily cron in `apps/worker/src/index.ts`. Change both together.
